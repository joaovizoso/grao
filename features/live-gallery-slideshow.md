# Live Gallery / Reception-Screen Slideshow

**Status:** deferred
**Plan tier:** New upsell add-on (alongside print-on-site / soundproof cabin), not baked into Basic/Premium.
**Proposed:** 2026-07-31 (architect-strategy)

## Summary

A second screen at the venue (TV/projector) cycling through submitted photos/boomerangs in near-real-time, as reception ambiance/entertainment.

## Why it's deferred (devil's advocate — architect-strategy)

Strongest candidate for "excitement dressed up as product thinking." Requires the same missing backend as QR/remote recording (see [qr-remote-recording.md](qr-remote-recording.md)), adds real-time performance/network risk during an actual wedding reception, and raises a consent question `CLAUDE.md` hasn't addressed anywhere: a guest recording a private message for the couple may not expect it broadcast live on a screen at the venue. No client has asked for this.

## Trigger to revisit

A second/third booking generates a real client request for this specific feature, and a consent model for live-broadcasting guest content has been worked out.

- 2026-07-31 — Proposed, deferred.
